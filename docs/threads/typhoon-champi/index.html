Typhoon Champi

A super typhoon that skirted Guam and is heading toward the northern Philippines and Taiwan, with its biggest impact expected late in the week of 19 October.

    NewIntensifying

    Super Typhoon Champi heads for the Philippines and Taiwan after skirting Guam

    The storm passed about 10 miles from Guam, cutting power to about half the island, then briefly reached super typhoon strength.

    Trump approved an emergency declaration for Guam on Saturday. Philippine forecasters expect Champi to enter their area of responsibility on Tuesday, where it will be called Rosal, and say it may re-intensify into a super typhoon; northern Luzon could see wind warnings. Taiwan's weather agency says it could issue a sea warning as early as Wednesday night, with the heaviest impact on Friday and Saturday, though models still disagree on whether the storm turns north before reaching the island.

    Why it matters. A storm of this strength on a track toward northern Luzon and Taiwan threatens dense coasts and key shipping and chip-making regions late this week.
