Typhoon Champi

A super typhoon that skirted Guam re-intensified to about 900 hPa on 20 October. It is forecast to pass near Japan's Sakishima Islands on the evening of 23 October, then turn north-east, possibly towards Kyushu; Taiwan expects heavy rain in the north and east on 23–24 October.
