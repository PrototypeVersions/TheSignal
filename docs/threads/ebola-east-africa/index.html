Ebola in East Africa

A Bundibugyo-strain outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo, with no approved vaccine or treatment, has passed 9,400 cases and 4,500 deaths across eight provinces; North Kivu has barred entry from Ituri. Kenya has eight cases, all linked to one imported patient.
