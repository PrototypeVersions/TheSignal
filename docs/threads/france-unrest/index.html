France unrest

A cost-of-living and schools protest movement turned into France's worst unrest in years on 17 October, with more than 2,500 people taken into custody and at least 3,000 shops damaged. Targeted curfews have brought calm nights; the government promises a massive deployment for protests on 24 October, and unions march on 5 November.
