Japan's MMX mission to Mars's moons

JAXA's MMX probe launched on 19 October and is bound for Mars, arriving in August 2027. It is due to land twice on Phobos and return about 10 grams of soil to Earth in 2031, aiming to settle whether Mars's moons are captured asteroids or debris from a giant impact.
