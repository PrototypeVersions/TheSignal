Brazil's presidential election

Senator Flávio Bolsonaro and President Lula face a runoff on 25 October. Flávio's narrow polling lead is shrinking, and the last debate is on 23 October.

DevelopingHoldingas of 19 Oct 2026

  1. Monday 19 October 2026
    DevelopingHolding

    Brazil's runoff tightens after a chaotic first debate

    Four polls released since Friday put the race within about two points either way, and one shows Lula ahead.

    Counting valid votes only, Ipespe gives Flávio Bolsonaro 51.2% to Lula's 48.8%, Veritá 51.1% to 48.9%, and Nexus/BTG 50.3% to 49.7%, while Vox Brasil has Lula ahead 51% to 49%, according to the Rio Times poll tracker. All are within or near their margins of error. Analysts called Sunday's Band TV debate chaotic and split on the winner. The last debate, on TV Globo, is on Friday night.

    Why it matters. Flávio's narrow lead from the first round is shrinking, and with six days to go neither side can count on it.

  2. Sunday 18 October 2026
    DevelopingHolding

    Brazil's runoff is a coin toss, one week out

    Three polls give Senator Flávio Bolsonaro a one- to two-point edge over President Lula, inside every margin of error.

    Flávio, 42, is the son of former president Jair Bolsonaro, who is under house arrest and barred from office until 2030. He led the 4 October first round with about 56.1 million votes to Lula's 53.9 million. The latest runoff polls: CNT/MDA 46.8% to 45.9%, Quaest 46% to 44%, Datafolha 47% to 45%.

    Why it matters. The vote sets the direction of Latin America's largest economy. With a margin this thin, the days after the vote may matter as much as the vote itself.
