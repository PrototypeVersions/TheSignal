    Brazil's runoff is a coin toss, one week out

    Three polls give Senator Flávio Bolsonaro a one- to two-point edge over President Lula, inside every margin of error.

    Flávio, 42, is the son of former president Jair Bolsonaro, who is under house arrest and barred from office until 2030. He led the 4 October first round with about 56.1 million votes to Lula's 53.9 million. The latest runoff polls: CNT/MDA 46.8% to 45.9%, Quaest 46% to 44%, Datafolha 47% to 45%.

    Why it matters. The vote sets the direction of Latin America's largest economy. With a margin this thin, the days after the vote may matter as much as the vote itself.
