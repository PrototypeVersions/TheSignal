North America

  • Alberta referendumMajor shift20 OctOn 19 October Albertans rejected, by about 76% to 24% in early counts, starting a process toward a binding referendum on leaving Canada. Official results for all ten questions are due on 21 October; Premier Smith has ruled out a follow-up separation vote in her mandate, but separatists vow to continue.
  • US midterms 2026Developing20 OctControl of Congress is decided on 3 November. Trump's approval has fallen to 31% in Reuters/Ipsos polling, Democrats lead the generic ballot by up to 12 points, and early voting has opened in record numbers.

Latin America

  • Brazil's presidential electionDeveloping19 OctSenator Flávio Bolsonaro and President Lula face a runoff on 25 October. Flávio's narrow polling lead is shrinking, and the last debate is on 23 October.

Europe

  • France unrestDeveloping20 OctA cost-of-living and schools protest movement turned into France's worst unrest in years on 17 October, with more than 2,500 people taken into custody. Targeted curfews in Paris and other cities brought a calm night on 19–20 October; clashes have also hit Milan, and new protests are called for 24 October.
  • Russia–Ukraine warDeveloping20 OctThe full-scale war is in its fifth year. Both sides are trading mass strikes on each other's energy systems as winter nears, Washington has paused new sanctions on Russia for up to 60 days, and Trump now says someone else should negotiate for Ukraine; a Trump–Putin–Xi meeting is floated for APEC on 18–19 November.

Africa

  • Ebola in East AfricaDeveloping19 OctA Bundibugyo-strain outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo, with no approved vaccine or treatment, has passed 9,400 cases and 4,500 deaths across eight provinces; North Kivu has barred entry from Ituri. Kenya has eight cases, all linked to one imported patient.

Middle East

  • Iran war and the GulfDeveloping20 OctThe US–Iran war has run since late February, with a spring ceasefire that collapsed in July. Iran claims new mine strikes on tankers in Hormuz and its offer to reopen the strait still awaits a US answer; Brent is near $112. The Houthis are hitting Saudi Arabia and Yemen's government, killing 60 at an army camp on 19 October.
  • Israel's electionDeveloping20 OctIsrael votes on 27 October in wartime. Polls show both Netanyahu's bloc and Eisenkot's opposition at about 51 seats, far from 61; Trump has endorsed Netanyahu and Defence Secretary Hegseth visited a week before the vote.
  • West Bank violenceDeveloping20 OctSettler attacks during the olive harvest have killed at least four Palestinians and reached Western diplomats and a US journalist. Israeli police arrested 10 people on 19 October after public pressure from the US ambassador.

Asia

  • Typhoon ChampiDeveloping20 OctA super typhoon that skirted Guam is now forecast to turn north near Ishigaki from 22 October, sparing the northern Philippines and Taiwan a direct hit, though heavy rain is expected in northern and eastern Taiwan on 23–24 October.
  • Pakistan–Afghanistan borderDeveloping18 OctIntermittent fighting between Pakistani forces and the Afghan Taliban, alongside an emergency declared in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Oceania

  • Attack on an Australian MPDeveloping20 OctIndependent MP Helen Haines lost a leg and remains critical after a car hit her outside her Wangaratta office on 19 October.
