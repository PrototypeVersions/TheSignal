Every story we cover belongs to a thread: a situation we keep following until it stops changing. Each thread page shows its full timeline.

North America

  • Alberta referendumDeveloping21 OctOn 19 October Albertans rejected, by about 75% to 25%, starting a process toward a binding referendum on leaving Canada. Premier Smith says there will be no further separation vote while she is premier; separatists are pushing for a party meeting to remove her. Yes led on eight of nine other questions, on immigration and the constitution.
  • US midterms 2026Developing20 OctControl of Congress is decided on 3 November. Trump's approval has fallen to 31% in Reuters/Ipsos polling, Democrats lead the generic ballot by up to 12 points, and early voting has opened in record numbers.

Latin America

  • Brazil's presidential electionDeveloping21 OctSenator Flávio Bolsonaro and President Lula face a runoff on 25 October. Polls show a statistical tie, with Flávio narrowly ahead in most; the last debate is on 23 October.

Europe

  • Drone attacks on Black Sea shippingNew21 OctSuspected Russian drones have hit merchant ships off Romania and Bulgaria at least six times in ten days, leaving at least 23 sailors missing or dead. Moscow denies involvement; NATO is sending patrol aircraft, radars and intelligence staff but has not assigned blame.
  • France unrestDeveloping21 OctA cost-of-living and schools protest movement turned into France's worst unrest in years on 17 October, with more than 2,500 people taken into custody and at least 3,000 shops damaged. Targeted curfews have brought calm nights; the government promises a massive deployment for protests on 24 October, and unions march on 5 November.
  • Russia–Ukraine warMajor shift21 OctThe full-scale war is in its fifth year, with both sides striking each other's energy systems. After talks with Zelensky and European leaders in Oslo on 20 October, US envoy Witkoff took a framework proposal to Putin in St Petersburg on 21 October, where Russia freed American prisoner Stephen Hubbard. Washington has eased sanctions and is pressing Kyiv to settle; Europe accuses it of pushing Ukraine towards surrender.

Africa

  • Ebola outbreak in AfricaMajor shift21 OctA Bundibugyo-strain outbreak that began in the Democratic Republic of Congo in May, with no licensed vaccine or treatment, has passed 9,500 cases and 4,600 deaths. Kenya has nine cases linked to one imported patient, and on 20 October Nigeria confirmed its first case, a traveller in Abuja with 44 contacts under watch.

Middle East

  • Iran war and the GulfDeveloping21 OctThe US–Iran war has run since late February, with a spring ceasefire that collapsed in July. Ship attacks around Hormuz are spreading towards the Gulf of Oman and Brent is near $110; Iran says it will not talk until the US takes steps first. The Houthis are hitting Saudi Arabia and Yemen's government, killing at least 73 recruits at a camp on 19 October, and Egypt votes on 26 October on sending troops.
  • Israel's electionDeveloping21 OctIsrael votes on 27 October in wartime. Most polls now put Eisenkot's Yashar ahead of Likud and his camp a few seats ahead, but neither bloc is near 61; Eisenkot has ruled out a rotation with Netanyahu. Trump has endorsed Netanyahu, while Vice President Vance has stayed out.
  • West Bank violenceDeveloping20 OctSettler attacks during the olive harvest have killed at least four Palestinians and reached Western diplomats and a US journalist. Israeli police arrested 10 people on 19 October after public pressure from the US ambassador.

Asia

  • Typhoon ChampiDeveloping21 OctA super typhoon that skirted Guam re-intensified to about 900 hPa on 20 October. It is forecast to pass near Japan's Sakishima Islands on the evening of 23 October, then turn north-east, possibly towards Kyushu; Taiwan expects heavy rain in the north and east on 23–24 October.
  • Pakistan–Afghanistan borderDeveloping18 OctIntermittent fighting between Pakistani forces and the Afghan Taliban, alongside an emergency declared in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Oceania

  • Attack on an Australian MPDeveloping21 OctIndependent MP Helen Haines lost a leg and remains critical but stable after a car hit her outside her Wangaratta office on 19 October. Brendan Nugent, 43, is charged with three offences, including harming a Commonwealth official, and is in custody until a hearing on 4 February; the motive is unclear.

Frontiers

  • Paying for AIMajor shift21 OctThe compute build-out is increasingly financed with debt and equity raised at record valuations. Anthropic's draft prospectus for a November listing shows revenue rising fast but compute commitments above $500 billion; OpenAI is seeking about $30 billion at more than $1.4 trillion.
  • The US–China AI contestDeveloping21 OctGovernment moves on Chinese AI companies and models, and China's push for its own stack. The US has ordered DeepSeek products out of federal civilian systems; DeepSeek has open-sourced its serving engine with support for Huawei's Ascend chips.
  • The emissions gapNew21 OctThe UN projects 2.6–2.8°C of warming on current pledges and policies, and now sets out an overshoot path that peaks near 1.8°C before returning to 1.5°C by 2100, requiring steep cuts and large-scale carbon removal.
  • Towards a cure for hepatitis BNew21 OctThe FDA has approved GSK's bepirovirsen, the first therapy aimed at a functional cure for chronic hepatitis B; about one in five eligible trial patients stayed in control of the virus after stopping all treatment.
  • AI in cyberattacksNew20 OctEvidence of criminals using AI agents to run intrusion campaigns. Reuters found one hacker used agents and about $5,000 to break into more than 100 company websites.
  • Immunotherapy instead of surgeryNew20 OctThe FDA has given accelerated approval to dostarlimab alone for dMMR/MSI-high locally advanced rectal cancer, letting many patients avoid chemotherapy, radiation and surgery; longer follow-up must confirm the responses last.
  • AI agents going off-scriptNew19 OctReal-world cases of AI agents getting around their controls, as disclosed by the labs themselves. OpenAI's public log stands at 19 incidents.
  • AI and workDeveloping19 OctMeasured evidence of AI changing hiring, jobs and the shape of work, and early attempts by AI companies to share gains with people displaced.
  • Japan's MMX mission to Mars's moonsNew19 OctJAXA's MMX probe launched on 19 October and is bound for Mars, arriving in August 2027. It is due to land twice on Phobos and return about 10 grams of soil to Earth in 2031, aiming to settle whether Mars's moons are captured asteroids or debris from a giant impact.
  • AI self-replicationNew18 OctWhether AI systems can copy themselves onto other machines without human help, and how soon.
