    At one AI lab, junior hiring has collapsed

    Anthropic says junior roles fell from 27% to 9% of its technical hires.

    • Update, 5:58pm CT Anthropic said on Sunday it will put 5% of its shares into a public benefit fund, adding 2% for every 50% rise in its value, citing the pace of AI progress. Ideas include large nonprofit grants or direct payments to specific groups; governance details are due in the coming months.
      Sources (1)
      • Anthropic says it's committing 5% of its shares to a 'public benefit fund'TechCrunch

    The company's own report adds that by August, AI agents directed by individual employees were logging six times as many working hours as the people directing them.

    This changes the picture because It is one company, and an AI lab at that. But it is an early, measured case of the pattern economists worry about most: AI absorbing entry-level work first, and with it the usual path into a profession.
