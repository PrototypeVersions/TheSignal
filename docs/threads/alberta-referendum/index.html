Alberta referendum

On 19 October Albertans rejected, by about 76% to 24% in early counts, starting a process toward a binding referendum on leaving Canada. Official results for all ten questions are due on 21 October; Premier Smith has ruled out a follow-up separation vote in her mandate, but separatists vow to continue.
