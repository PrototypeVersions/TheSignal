    NewIntensifying

    Alberta votes tomorrow on starting the road out of Canada

    The last of ten questions on Monday's ballot asks whether to begin a process toward a binding separation referendum. Polls show staying in Canada far ahead.

    Angus Reid's final survey, taken 13–15 October, has 66% backing remaining a province and 34% for starting the separation process. Officials expect turnout above 80% of nearly three million registered voters, far higher than recent provincial elections.

    Most supporters of the separation option tell pollsters they mainly want to send Ottawa a message. Premier Danielle Smith has said there will be no further separation vote if this one fails; separatist organizers say their movement continues whatever the result.

    Why it matters. A lopsided loss would blunt the movement. A closer-than-polled result would keep a national-unity question alive into Alberta's 2027 election.
