Threads / Middle East

Iran war and the Gulf

The US–Iran war has run since late February, with a spring ceasefire that collapsed in July. Ship attacks around Hormuz are spreading towards the Gulf of Oman and Brent is near $110; Iran says it will not talk until the US takes steps first. The Houthis are hitting Saudi Arabia and Yemen's government, killing at least 73 recruits at a camp on 19 October, and Egypt votes on 26 October on sending troops.
