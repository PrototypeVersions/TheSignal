Attack on an Australian MP

Independent MP Helen Haines was critically injured when a car hit her outside her electorate office; the prime minister called it deliberate and counterterrorism police are investigating.

    Australian MP critically hurt in what Albanese calls a deliberate attack

    Independent MP Helen Haines was hit by a car outside her Wangaratta office on Monday. A 43-year-old man was arrested and the joint counterterrorism team has been activated.

    Bystanders detained the driver, and police found a knife in the vehicle, ABC News (Australia) reports. No charges have been reported so far, and the prime minister says it is too early to know the motive. The Home Affairs minister, Tony Burke, said federal police received 1,007 reports of threats and harassment against people in public roles in 2025–26, up from 951 the year before.

    Why it matters. Attacks on federal politicians are rare in Australia. Counterterrorism involvement and rising threat reports will put the security of MPs and their local offices on the national agenda.
