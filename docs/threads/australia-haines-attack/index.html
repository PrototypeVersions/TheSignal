Threads / Oceania

Attack on an Australian MP

Independent MP Helen Haines lost a leg and remains critical but stable after a car hit her outside her Wangaratta office on 19 October. Brendan Nugent, 43, is charged with three offences, including harming a Commonwealth official, and is in custody until a hearing on 4 February; the motive is unclear.
