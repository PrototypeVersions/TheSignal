The day, in view

Russia answered Ukraine's weekend drone raid with an overnight strike on the grid that cut power to 550,000 homes, while US sanctions on Moscow stay paused. In Yemen, a Houthi missile strike on an army camp killed at least 53 recruits, officials said by evening, and Saudi Arabia put off its flagship investment conference to 2027. France's prime minister asked towns to impose curfews, and cost-of-living clashes spread to Milan. Albertans finish voting today on whether to start down the road out of Canada, and Israel's election, eight days off, is deadlocked. This afternoon Japan launched MMX, a mission to bring back the first soil from a moon of Mars.
