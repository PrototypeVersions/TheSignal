Developing

North Kivu bars entry from Ebola-hit Ituri

Congo's North Kivu province has banned entry from neighbouring Ituri for a week from 19 October, limited vehicle passengers and suspended large gatherings. Ituri has nearly three-quarters of confirmed cases.

Army drone strike kills four civilians in West Kordofan

A Sudanese army drone hit a vehicle carrying patients near Ankoush, West Kordofan, on 17 October, killing four civilians, according to a local monitoring group.
