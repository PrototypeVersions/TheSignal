Chile's president makes the first state visit to Bolivia in 71 years

José Antonio Kast met Bolivia's president, Rodrigo Paz, in La Paz on 16 October. The two governments set a gradual path toward restoring full diplomatic relations, broken for more than 70 years, and Paz accepted an invitation to visit Chile in 2027.
