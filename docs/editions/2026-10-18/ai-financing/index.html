The AI build-out is running on more expensive debt

Anthropic has offered a 10% coupon on $9 billion of junior debt, and OpenAI's ad business looks set to miss its target.

The Signal · Sunday 18 October 2026 · Updated 5:58pm CT

Since this morning

  • Update, 5:58pm CT

    Microsoft has pulled out as anchor tenant of Nscale's planned data center in Loughton, England, the Telegraph reports; the site may not get a grid connection until the 2030s. Nscale, which is preparing a stock listing and says Microsoft and Anthropic make up about 85% of its contracts, says the relationship remains strong.

    Sources (1)
    • Microsoft pulls out of UK data centre deal with Nscale, Telegraph reportsReuters via Investing.com

According to the Financial Times, Anthropic offered Blackstone 10% on $9 billion of junior debt within a $60 billion financing for Google-designed chips; four months ago it borrowed about $4.4 billion at 8.5%. The Information reports that OpenAI told investors to expect $2.4 billion in advertising revenue this year and is likely to fall short.

Why it matters

Frontier compute is now financed like power plants, at rising rates each round. That ties the pace of AI progress to credit markets and to revenue, not only to research.
