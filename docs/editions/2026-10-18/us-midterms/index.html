DevelopingUS midterms 2026

Trump's approval hits a record low with 16 days to go

A Washington Post–ABC–Ipsos poll puts his approval at 33% among all adults and 38% among likely voters.

The Signal · Sunday 18 October 2026

Approval is down four points since July and eight from a year ago. Only 28% approve of his handling of the economy, and about three in four disapprove of how he has handled inflation and the cost of living. Democrats lead by eight points on which party Americans want running the House, up five since July.

Why it matters

It is one poll, but it lands as fuel prices climb toward election day, and the economy, usually a Republican strength, is now the president's weakest number.

What to watch

Early-vote turnout, and whether other high-quality polls show the same drop.
