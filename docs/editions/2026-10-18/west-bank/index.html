DevelopingWest Bank violence

Settlers attack European diplomats as Washington tells Israel to crack down

Masked men with metal bars attacked a delegation including Belgium's consul general and French and Polish diplomats in the West Bank village of Taybeh on Saturday.

The Signal · Sunday 18 October 2026 · Updated 5:58pm CT

The attack came a day after a Washington Post photographer, Heidi Levine, was seriously injured in another assault. The Palestinian Health Ministry says at least four people have been killed and more than 100 injured since the olive harvest began this month.

Israel's army called the attack a severe act of violence and police opened an investigation; Foreign Minister Gideon Saar condemned it, while Netanyahu's office warned against premature conclusions. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said he was very disturbed by the violence and wants Israel to crack down.

Why it matters

Attacks on Western diplomats and journalists, together with open US criticism, raise the diplomatic cost for Israel less than two weeks before a closely fought Israeli election.

What to watch

Arrests in the Taybeh and Levine cases; any European move toward sanctions on violent settlers.

Since this morning

  • Update, 5:58pm CT

    As of Sunday afternoon there were still no arrests over the Taybeh attack or the assault on Heidi Levine. The EU demanded full accountability, Belgium said it would keep pushing for EU sanctions on violent settlers, and Israeli police say the European missions have not yet handed over the evidence investigators requested.
