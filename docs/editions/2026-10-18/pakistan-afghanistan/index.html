DevelopingPakistan–Afghanistan border

Pakistan and the Taliban keep trading fire on the border

Pakistani officials say artillery-backed troops destroyed Afghan Taliban posts on Saturday, as clashes that began two weeks ago show no sign of stopping.

The Signal · Sunday 18 October 2026

Fighting has flared on and off since 5 October, when regional talks in Moscow ended without a joint statement. Islamabad accuses the Taliban of sheltering the Pakistani Taliban and other militant groups; a suicide bombing in the northwest in September killed 31 people.

At home, President Zardari declared an emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa on 11 October and suspended its provincial government, which is led by Imran Khan's party.

Why it matters

A slow-burning border war is merging with a political crisis in Pakistan's most volatile province.

What to watch

Whether strikes move beyond border posts, and how Imran Khan's party responds to losing its provincial government.
