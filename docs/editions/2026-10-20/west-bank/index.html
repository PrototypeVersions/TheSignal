DevelopingWest Bank violence

Israeli police arrest 10 after settlers attack diplomats and a journalist

The arrests followed weekend attacks on a Washington Post photographer and on European diplomats visiting the village of Taybeh, which the US ambassador called terror.

The Signal · Tuesday 20 October 2026

Key facts

  • Police arrested 10 people on Monday in an operation with the Shin Bet, Border Police and army; suspects were not named.
  • Washington Post photographer Heidi Levine suffered broken ribs and vertebrae when settlers attacked her on 16 October.
  • Masked settlers attacked Belgian, French and Polish diplomats in Taybeh on 17 October.
  • Palestinian health officials say at least four Palestinians have been killed and more than 100 injured since the olive harvest began.

The arrests

Israeli police said on Monday they had arrested 10 people over the wave of settler violence in the occupied West Bank, in an operation with the Shin Bet security service, the Border Police and the army. The suspects were not identified. Police said the operation also targeted breaches of closed military zone orders and illegal construction, and that several vehicles were seized, AP reports. US Ambassador Mike Huckabee called the arrests a very positive development, according to Al Jazeera.

The attacks

On Friday a group of young settlers attacked Heidi Levine, a photographer for the Washington Post, in the village of Abu Falah northeast of Ramallah. She suffered broken ribs and vertebrae and a nearly severed finger. From hospital she criticised Netanyahu for describing the attack as alleged.

A day later, masked settlers attacked a group visiting the Christian village of Taybeh that included Belgium's consul general, French and Polish diplomats and Catholic representatives. Huckabee called it a despicable act of terror. Belgium's foreign minister, Maxime Prévot, said impunity for violent settlers must end.

The harvest season

The attacks came during the olive harvest, on which about 110,000 Palestinian families depend. The Palestinian Health Ministry says at least four Palestinians have been killed and more than 100 injured since the harvest began in early October, and the UN humanitarian office counted more than 1,600 settler attacks from January to August. Rights groups say attackers are rarely charged.

Why it matters

Attacks on foreign diplomats and a US journalist have drawn rare public pressure from Washington, and the arrests are the first sign of an Israeli response. Whether anyone is charged will show if it marks a change.

What to watch

Whether the 10 are charged or released; diplomatic steps by Belgium, France, Poland and the EU; further attacks as the harvest runs into November.
