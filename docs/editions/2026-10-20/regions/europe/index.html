New

Hoax bomb threats hit hundreds of European schools

Up to 300 schools in Northern Ireland, more than 100 in Serbia and more than 70 in Hungary received emailed bomb threats on Monday; some were evacuated, and police treated them as hoaxes. It is not known whether the incidents are linked.

Albania's protests reach a seventh day

Protesters threw stones and petrol bombs at the Interior Ministry in Tirana and damaged the Court of Appeal; police used tear gas and water cannon and arrested five. Prime Minister Edi Rama offered talks but refuses to resign.
