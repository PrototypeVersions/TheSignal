NAB plans to cut up to 2,500 jobs

National Australia Bank said it will cut up to 2,500 jobs, about 6% of staff, by mid-2027. Its announcement did not mention AI, though senators are pressing the bank on whether automation is a factor.

Cyclone Wati damages a new wharf in the Cook Islands

The cyclone passed over Aitutaki on 18 October as a Category 2 storm, causing major damage to the $7 million Arutanga wharf; no injuries were reported, and it weakened as it moved toward French Polynesia.
