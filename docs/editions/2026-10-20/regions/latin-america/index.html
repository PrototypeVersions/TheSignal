New

Four Salvadoran troops missing after gang attack in Haiti

Gangs attacked an armoured vehicle of the UN-backed Gang Suppression Force near Port-au-Prince with explosives and set it on fire; the four Salvadoran airmen escaped but are unaccounted for. It is the biggest attack on the force since it began operations.

Illegal gold mine collapse kills 14 in Colombia

A collapse at an informal mine in Santa Bárbara de Iscuandé, Nariño, killed 14 people and injured seven. The governor urged faster formalisation of small-scale mining.

Venezuela's opposition reaffirms Machado as leader

The opposition alliance recognised the exiled Nobel laureate María Corina Machado as its leader and set out 14 demands, including an election roadmap and release of political prisoners, during US-backed talks with the interim government.
