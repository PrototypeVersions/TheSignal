Joseph Kabila now heads Congo's main rebel coalition

Congo's former president leads a new political-military coalition, the Front for the Refoundation of the Nation, announced in rebel-held Goma. It replaces the alliance that included the M23 and calls for resistance to President Félix Tshisekedi, including by force.

New

Ethiopia puts Tigray under a federal interim government

Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed announced on 16 October that a five-member interim administration will run Tigray for six months, with elections promised afterward, following the defeat of the TPLF uprising.

New

Kenyan court convicts cult leader Paul Mackenzie of 191 murders

On Monday the High Court found Mackenzie and his co-accused guilty over the deaths of 191 people, mostly children, in the Shakahola forest, where followers were urged to starve. Sentencing has not been scheduled publicly.
