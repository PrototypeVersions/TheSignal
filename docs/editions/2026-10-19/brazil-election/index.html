DevelopingBrazil's presidential election

Brazil's runoff tightens after a chaotic first debate

Four polls released since Friday put the race within about two points either way, and one shows Lula ahead.

The Signal · Monday 19 October 2026

Key facts

  • Four polls since Friday put the runoff within about two points; Vox Brasil shows Lula ahead 51% to 49%.
  • Datafolha's first post-first-round poll had Flávio Bolsonaro on 49% and Lula on 45%, a technical tie.
  • The last debate, on TV Globo, is on Friday 23 October.
  • Brazilians vote on 25 October.

A race that keeps narrowing

Flávio Bolsonaro, the eldest son of former president Jair Bolsonaro, unexpectedly finished ahead of President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva in the first round, and the first Datafolha poll afterwards gave him 49% to Lula's 45%, within the margin of error.

Six days before the runoff that edge is shrinking. Counting valid votes only, Ipespe gives Flávio 51.2% to Lula's 48.8%, Veritá 51.1% to 48.9% and Nexus/BTG 50.3% to 49.7%, while Vox Brasil has Lula ahead by 51% to 49%, according to the Rio Times poll tracker. All four are within or near their margins of error.

The campaign

Sunday's debate on Band TV was widely described as chaotic, and analysts were split on who won. In a TV Globo interview last week, Flávio promised a fiscal adjustment of 1.5% to 2% of GDP, funded by selling public property, securitising debts and fighting corruption, while ruling out cuts to the real minimum-wage increase, pension floors and health and education spending. He called the end of the six-day working week opportunistic but said he would not reverse it.

What comes next

Watch the Datafolha and AtlasIntel polls this week, the final debate on TV Globo on Friday night, and the vote on Sunday 25 October.

Why it matters

Flávio's narrow lead from the first round is shrinking, and with six days to go neither side can count on it.

What to watch

Datafolha and AtlasIntel polls this week; the Globo debate on 23 October; the vote on 25 October.
