Australian MP critically hurt in what Albanese calls a deliberate attack

Independent MP Helen Haines was hit by a car outside her Wangaratta office on Monday. A 43-year-old man was arrested and the joint counterterrorism team has been activated.

The Signal · Monday 19 October 2026

Key facts

  • Independent MP Helen Haines was critically injured when she was hit by a car outside her Wangaratta office on Monday.
  • A 43-year-old man was arrested; police found a knife in the vehicle.
  • The joint counterterrorism team has been activated; no charges have been reported.
  • Federal police received 1,007 reports of threats and harassment against people in public roles in 2025–26, up from 951.

What happened

Helen Haines, an independent member of Australia's federal parliament, was hit by a car outside her office in Wangaratta, Victoria, on Monday and critically injured. Bystanders detained the driver, a 43-year-old man, and police found a knife in his vehicle, ABC News (Australia) reports. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese called it a deliberate attack.

The investigation

The joint counterterrorism team has been activated. No charges had been reported by Monday evening, and the prime minister said it was too early to know the motive.

Threats against public figures

Attacks on federal politicians are rare in Australia. The Home Affairs minister, Tony Burke, said federal police received 1,007 reports of threats and harassment against people in public roles in 2025–26, up from 951 the year before. Watch for charges and any finding on motive, and for changes to security at MPs' local offices, which are usually open to the public.

Why it matters

Attacks on federal politicians are rare in Australia. Counterterrorism involvement and rising threat reports will put the security of MPs and their local offices on the national agenda.

What to watch

Charges and any finding on motive; changes to security for electorate offices.
